# Primary-source comparison for the focused papers

Checked 18 October 2026. This is a bounded submission-facing comparison of definitions, claims and proof scope, not a declaration of historical priority or an exhaustive review of every graph partition function. References were consulted through primary journal records, institutional author records or author-posted manuscripts. The source links permit direct reassessment.

Its title, three authors and lattice-model scope agree with `docs/references.bib`. This closes the old unresolved BPR bibliographic check. Searches included the exact titles above and the concepts integer-valued graph Gaussian models, graph height partition functions and Ising-equivalent graph refinements. The comparison found relevant established frameworks and supports the narrower claims in the manuscript. It cannot establish absence of a prior identical construction; that conclusion is not used for roadmap acceptance.

## Second paper: established coefficient machinery and the selected theorem

Patel–Regts2017 [arXiv:1607.01167v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.01167v3), Section3, develops low-order coefficient computation from connected induced counts; Lemma3.5 identifies additivity with support on connected graphs, and Theorem3.7 treats vertex/edge colors. The general coefficient and coloring principles are therefore attributed. The selected paper specializes the formal support-log calculation to integer heights with ambient degree labels, then proves a sharp inverse theorem rather than claiming a new origin for connected-cluster expansion.

Patel–Regts2019 [arXiv:1707.05186](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.05186) gives induced-copy computation on bounded-degree graphs. Its primary [author-institution publication record](https://dare.uva.nl/id/216e66de-d810-4d4e-8960-ce404942dc51) verifies *Algorithmica*81(5),1844–1858,2019 and DOI10.1007/s00453-018-0511-9. The project benchmark compares its own two exact implementations, not this broader algorithmic literature.

Chertkov–Chernyak2006 [arXiv:cond-mat/0603189](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0603189) and the [author-hosted journal manuscript](https://cnls.lanl.gov/~chertkov/Papers/06CCb.pdf) verify *Loop series for discrete statistical models on graphs*, J.Stat.Mech.(2006)P06009, DOI10.1088/1742-5468/2006/06/P06009. Their belief-propagation plus generalized-loop decomposition is neighboring context, not the support-log identity's normalization. This distinction is explicit in the second paper.

The second paper's narrow center is its explicit height-energy cycle coefficient, unit-triangular preservation under the declared closures, and sharp floor(n/2) reconstruction for simple 2-regular interiors of known order. Those results have complete proofs in the article and finite independent receipts. Neither priority over all graph-invariant work nor unrestricted completeness of fixed pinning is asserted.
