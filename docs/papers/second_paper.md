# Selected second paper

The selected and completed manuscript is [Exact observation depth for integer-height graph populations](locality.md). Its center is sharp reconstruction of every simple 2-regular graph of known order n at total-height budget floor(n/2), with explicit sharpness pairs and a connected-support compiler.

The [selection decision](second_paper_choice.md) compares the achieved alternatives, verified primary literature and remaining questions. The [claim register](locality_claims.md) gives theorem scope, exact receipts and independent review. Generated delivery files are [PDF](../../output/pdf/locality_paper.pdf), [HTML](../locality_paper.html) and [LaTeX](../locality_paper.tex). The complete release archive includes source, tests and retained finite data.
