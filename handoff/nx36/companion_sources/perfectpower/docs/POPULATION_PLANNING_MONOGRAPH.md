# Exact population planning by bounded residual polynomials

The October 12, 2026 extension closes the executable Petersen 50-project population problem. The computed feasible population is **702,508,860,122,761 original project selections**. The maximum objective is **16,537**, with **one** maximizing selection. The earlier optimization-only witness established neither of these population counts. This extension constructs counts for a disjoint cover of the original binary cube and supports original-coordinate conditional counts, lexicographic selection, inverse ranking and uniform rank-based sampling. Its native arithmetic and certificate replay are tested. A subsequent [standalone Boolean decision-diagram verification](INDEPENDENT_PLANNING_MONOGRAPH.md) independently reproduces the unrestricted total and all 176 retained root-leaf counts, and separately confirms the unique optimizer. Neither counting executable is proved in Lean, and no second externally authored solver has completed the unrestricted total in the retained external runs.

The route is explicitly selected with `strategy: "population"`. Automatic routing remains unchanged because the new machinery loses to the existing MITM implementation on the small retained complete-query workflow. Extending the original MITM variable cap would have created two populations with up to 33,554,432 records each without solving the expensive high-dimensional join. The new route instead combines heavy-project conditioning, exact suffix polynomial folds, resource implication, whole-subcube contraction and bounded memoization. Resource failures create smaller disjoint questions; they never become empty populations.

**Population semantics.** Let the named original projects be indexed by \(j=0,\ldots,n-1\), let \(c_j\in\mathbb N^m\) be their resource columns and let \(p_j\in\mathbb Q\) be their profits. For inclusive resource bounds \(\ell,u\), the population is
\[
\mathcal F(\ell,u)=\{x\in\{0,1\}^n:\ell_k\leq\sum_jc_{jk}x_j\leq u_k\text{ for every }k\}.
\]
The original identity of every project is retained. Internal permutations change evaluation order only. Fixed-coordinate subproblems carry two original-bit masks, a set of fixed positions and their values. An original prefix fixes its first coordinates, regardless of the internal search order. Distinct projects with equal columns remain distinct choices. Coincident polynomial exponents carry multiplicity rather than being deduplicated. Rational objectives are scaled by their common denominator; the existing native guard bounds the sum of absolute scaled profits by \(2^{60}\). Counts and tie counts use unsigned 128-bit arithmetic, including the \(2^{64}\) boundary allowed by the 64-project interface.

For remaining projects \(R\), the multivariate generating polynomial is
\[
P_R(z)=\prod_{j\in R}(1+z_1^{c_{j1}}\cdots z_m^{c_{jm}}).
\]
Summing its coefficients over the residual resource interval gives the remaining population. The implementation does not materialize this potentially enormous multivariate coefficient grid. Acyclic deletion/contraction evaluates selected portions, while low-dimensional suffix tables supply exact terminal aggregates. The same fold carries the feasible count, maximum objective, number of maximizing original assignments and smallest maximizing original mask. Disjoint union adds counts; its maximum is the larger branch maximum; ties add only across branches attaining that maximum. Selecting a project translates resource use and profit and sets its original mask bit.

**Canonical residual states and whole cubes.** At each suffix, let \(T_k\) be its maximum possible use of resource \(k\), the sum of its remaining nonnegative columns. A residual upper bound above \(T_k\) can be clipped to \(T_k\), and a residual lower bound above \(T_k\) proves emptiness. The suffix gcd \(g_k\) implies that all attainable totals are multiples of \(g_k\); the interval endpoints can therefore be rounded inward to those multiples. Zero-cost coordinates need no division. These operations preserve the exact accepted assignments and allow otherwise different slack vectors to share a memo key.

If every residual lower bound is zero and every upper bound admits \(T_k\), every remaining bit assignment is feasible. A suffix of \(r\) projects contributes \(2^r\) plans immediately. Its unconstrained maximum selects all positive-profit projects, rejects all negative-profit projects, and permits either value of every zero-profit project. If there are \(z\) zero-profit projects, the maximizing population has size \(2^z\). The minimum original maximizing mask sets no zero-profit bit. This contraction does not enumerate those plans or lose their identities.

**Exact resource implication.** For two resources \(k,h\), suffix \(R\), and a capacity \(b\), compute the ordinary one-resource knapsack bound
\[
M^R_{k\mid h}(b)=\max\{\sum_{j\in R}c_{jk}x_j:\sum_{j\in R}c_{jh}x_j\leq b,\ x\in\{0,1\}^R\}.
\]
The suffix dynamic program evaluates this maximum with exact nonnegative integers. If a state has lower bound zero on resource \(k\) and \(M^R_{k\mid h}(u_h)\leq u_k\), constraint \(k\) is implied by retained constraint \(h\). Its upper bound may be replaced by the unconstrained suffix total. No objective-profit relaxation or numerical tolerance enters this test. Constraints are removed sequentially, and a previously removed coordinate is not reused as an active anchor. Reversing this elimination sequence proves that the final retained constraints imply all removed ones. This handles conditional redundancy that identical-row or identical-project symmetry cannot detect.

When only one resource remains active, a stored univariate suffix polynomial supplies the exact aggregate. Its exact-cost buckets preserve coefficients and maximizing ties; prefix aggregates answer upper-only queries in constant time. Lower-bound intervals aggregate the relevant exact-cost buckets. For at most two active upper-only resources, bounded bivariate suffix tables use the recurrence
\[
A_j(a,b)=A_{j+1}(a,b)\oplus\bigl(p_j+A_{j+1}(a-c_{j1},b-c_{j2})\bigr),
\]
with the selected branch present only when both residual capacities admit the project. Capacities are clipped to the next suffix totals. The addition symbol denotes disjoint population aggregation, not numerical maximization alone. Two-resource tables are limited to the last 24 internally ordered projects and are created only when their estimated storage fits their share of the arena. Other states continue through the exact residual recurrence. This is partial variable elimination, with an explicit decision to avoid the prohibitively large five-resource grid.

**Heavy-project decomposition and bounded execution.** The 50-project data contains a few columns much larger than most others. Once these expensive decisions are fixed, large suffixes can be contracted or reduced to one or two active resources. The native fold orders free projects by a deterministic normalized resource weight. The Python coordinator uses the same ordering to split a failed subproblem on its largest remaining original project. For a fixed assignment \(q\) and a free original coordinate \(j\),
\[
\mathcal F(q)=\mathcal F(q,x_j=0)\;\dot\cup\;\mathcal F(q,x_j=1).
\]
This identity preserves project semantics and supplies the global counting equation even when the original residual cache is too large. The final root partition can be replayed by checking every split bit, both child masks, original-mask domains, leaf cube sizes and all internal count/maximum/tie aggregations. Summing the sizes of the root leaf cubes must give exactly \(2^{50}\). This proves disjoint coverage of the input cube by the retained partition; it does not replace validation of native computations inside its leaves.

The default native arena is 128 MiB, selectable from 1 MiB to 1 GiB. It backs memo nodes, keys, hash buckets and all substantial one- and two-resource polynomial storage through a monotonic allocator with no upstream fallback. Small fixed-dimension metadata, the interpreter and the bounded Python partition are outside this arena and are included in worker RSS measurements. The native memo-state limit defaults to 1,000,000; zero disables memo retention. The partition limit defaults to 8,192 retained answer/split nodes. A cumulative per-query work limit defaults to 2,000,000,000 evaluated setup cells and search visits. The existing `native_visit_limit` bounds each native attempt; setup cells and search visits share that attempt budget. The retained benchmark uses a 10,000,000-unit attempt limit. Failed attempts consume the cumulative budget. No partial population or incumbent maximum is returned through the complete-query API. Budget exhaustion exposes `WorkLimit`, with native work diagnostics where available.

Conditional queries intersect their original prefix with the retained heavy-project tree, reject conflicting branches, and evaluate only compatible residual leaves. Complete native leaf answers and split decisions can be reused. Result dictionaries are copied on return. The finite partition receipt retains original fixed masks and exact answers rather than treating the search permutation as the public order.

**Ranks, inverse ranks and sampling.** Write \(C(q)\) for the exact number of completions of an original prefix. Lexicographic selection compares a rank with \(C(q0)\): it descends to zero if the rank is smaller, otherwise subtracts that count and descends to one. Inverse ranking sums zero-branch populations skipped by the original tuple. Induction on the remaining prefix length, together with \(C(q)=C(q0)+C(q1)\), proves that these maps are inverse when the supplied counts are exact. Uniform integer rank selection consequently assigns probability \(1/|\mathcal F|\) to each original feasible tuple. Global-optimal selection uses a branch's tie count only when its conditional maximum equals the global maximum. A conditional optimum below that value contributes no globally maximizing plans.

The retained complete bank contains endpoint, quartile and middle ranks, independently recomputed inverse ranks, original-prefix counts, the maximizing plan, and a seeded sample. Inverse-ranking tests explicitly clear the shortcut written by selection, so a cached rank cannot masquerade as a verification of the count recurrence. Independent original-order recursion checks selected prefixes leaving 24 variables, including conditional maxima and tie counts. Separate small exhaustive tests cover lower bounds, zero costs, negative and fractional profits, empty populations, ties, cache copies, work failures and the 64-variable count boundary.

**Independent encodings and comparisons.** `planning_cnf.py` constructs a second mathematical representation: resource sums as functional ripple-adder circuits, followed by exact binary comparators for inclusive bounds. Every gate is constrained in both directions by its complete truth table. Each original project assignment therefore has exactly one extension to gate variables. An unprojected #SAT count equals the original feasible population; auxiliary gate choices cannot multiply the result. The 50-project encoding has 3,952 variables and 17,373 clauses before original-prefix units. Exhaustive small differential tests check every original assignment's extension and use the separate CNF counter to check both total populations and unique conditional extensions.

The pinned external reference is Ganak 2.8.0, binary SHA-256 `934a79a3db2f5b3928ce89edde20837f652e4679b099320ac71c198699c709dd`, using `--prob 0`, integer unweighted counting and the `--maxcache 128` setting (documented in MB). No approximate fallback or projected population is used. External #SAT time limits and the 1 GiB process address-space ceiling are recorded explicitly. The retained PerfectPower CNF engine is a further counting baseline, with its own work-limit outcome. Both are counting comparisons; the independent OR-Tools and native knapsack optimizer measurements remain optimization-only.

Benchmark receipts live in `receipts/planning/population`. Each comparison worker starts with a fresh interpreter and records compiler/load time, cold population construction, the first complete query bank, twenty sustained repetitions of that bank, and peak worker RSS. Sustained repetitions reuse conditional answers; they are not a claim about arbitrary previously unseen queries. Compiler-child RSS is excluded. Independent workers ran on the same shared eight-CPU environment; CPU exclusivity was not enforced, so the measurements are scoped observations rather than controlled universal speed ratios. The prior release receipts are preserved without edits.

This advance closes the executable count, conditional-query, ranking and maximizer-tie gap for this retained instance. It does not prove general polynomial-time counting, discover unverified project symmetries, formally verify the native allocator or fold in Lean, or establish an unrestricted industrial advantage. The explicit partition and generating-function contract provide concrete future formalization targets. The measurements also show why a portfolio should retain the older small-instance routes.

## Subsequent independent unrestricted confirmation

The independently structured ROBDD route reads the original public constraints, constructs and intersects five threshold Boolean functions, and counts assignments with explicit skipped-coordinate multiplicities. It reproduces 702,508,860,122,761 before querying the original partition; all 176 terminal populations agree. Original-prefix rebuilds independently return 16,777,216, 512 and 16,095,745. A separate integer-bounded target enumerator finds exactly one feasible assignment at or above objective 16,537. The [new monograph](INDEPENDENT_PLANNING_MONOGRAPH.md) and [source-bound receipt](../receipts/planning/independent/50_bdd_nodes40000000.json) document the algorithms, limits and formal boundary. These close unrestricted independent implementation agreement without changing or reinterpreting any earlier solver timeout or benchmark.

The subsequent [59-theorem Lean extension](PLANNING_LEAN_MONOGRAPH.md) proves the diagram mathematics, two original-model conditional populations, optimizer witness arithmetic, and the literal partition coverage and aggregation identity. All new theorems passed the pinned kernel and axiom inspection. The full unrestricted native result still requires its concrete DAG correspondence and weight to be checked in Lean.
