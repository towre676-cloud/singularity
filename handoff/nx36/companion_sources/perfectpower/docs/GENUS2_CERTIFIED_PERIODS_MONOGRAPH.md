# Certified periods, an integral cycle map and node monodromy for split genus-two curves

This chapter delivers Priority 6 of the content handoff: a certified period packet for explicit split genus-two curves. The numerical surface layer and the elliptic quotient compiler already existed, but their period comparisons carried no certified error bound, and a quotient period was not tied to a marked genus-two cycle. Here every period is an Arb ball with a proven radius. The quotient maps act on homology through a certified integer matrix, the induced polarization identity holds exactly, the normalized period matrix is rebuilt from the two elliptic lattices alone, and the logarithmic monodromy at a node is identified. Its coefficient is twice the naive Picard–Lefschetz value, because the parameter loop induces a squared half-twist.

## The curves and their quotients

For rational 0<u1<u2<u3, put Q(u)=(u-u1)(u-u2)(u-u3) and

```text
C:  y^2 = P(z) = Q(z^2),       branch points ±sqrt(u1), ±sqrt(u2), ±sqrt(u3),
E1: v^2 = Q(u),                (u,v) = (z^2, y),
E2: w^2 = u Q(u),              (u,w) = (z^2, z y),
phi1^*(du/v) = 2 z dz/y,       phi2^*(du/w) = 2 dz/y.
```

These are the translated-even quotients of the curve-structure chapter, with h=0. The packet covers y^2=(z^2-1)(z^2-4)(z^2-9) and y^2=(z^2-2)(z^2-3)(z^2-7).

## Certified cycles

Order the branch points e_1<...<e_6. Let gamma_j be the closed lift of a loop around [e_j,e_{j+1}]. Its period is 2 times the integral from e_j to e_{j+1} of omega/y_u, where y_u is the upper-half-plane boundary value of the branch y~z^3 at infinity. On that segment y_u=i^{6-j} sqrt|P|. The substitution z=a+(b-a)(1-cos t)/2 turns each integral into one over [0,pi] whose integrand is analytic on a neighbourhood of the path, so acb.integral returns a rigorous enclosure. At 200 bits the radii are about 10^-58.

Path independence is checked by a second, independent evaluation. A clockwise circle around each segment is integrated with y continued analytically. On it, (z-m)sqrt(1-h^2/(z-m)^2) replaces the two enclosed factors, and each other factor uses a branch analytic on the disc. All ten (cycle, form) pairs agree with the segment values to at most 3.6 times 10^-57 on both curves.

## Riemann relations and the symplectic basis

With the chain intersections gamma_j·gamma_{j+1}=sigma, take a1=gamma1, a2=gamma3, b1=gamma2+gamma4 and b2=gamma4. This basis is symplectic for sigma=+1. Write A and B for the matrices of eta_0=dz/y and eta_1=z dz/y over the a- and b-cycles, and put Omega=A^{-1}B. The certified results are:

```text
y^2=(z^2-1)(z^2-4)(z^2-9):
  Omega = i [[1.78056882835559397832..., 0.89028441417779698916...],
             [0.89028441417779698916..., 1.16854756925055897997...]]
  Omega12-Omega21 in [+/- 3.6e-58],  tr Im Omega = 2.94911639760615...,  det Im Omega = 1.28807303813034...
y^2=(z^2-2)(z^2-3)(z^2-7):
  Omega = i [[1.47051185719822325498..., 0.73525592859911162749...],
             [0.73525592859911162749..., 0.85260847239175753427...]]
  tr Im Omega = 2.32312032958998...,  det Im Omega = 0.71316958765960...
```

Both Riemann relations are certified: Omega is symmetric, and Im Omega is positive definite. Positivity fixes the orientation sigma=+1.

## The integral cycle map

The pullbacks identify 2 times the eta_1-period of gamma_j with an element of the E1 lattice, and 2 times the eta_0-period with an element of the E2 lattice. The E_k lattices are generated by their own segment cycles, around [u1,u2] and [u2,u3] for E1, and around [0,u1] and [u1,u2] for E2. Solving the 2 by 2 real ball system gives coordinate intervals, each of which contains exactly one integer. The images of gamma_1,...,gamma_5 are therefore certified:

```text
E1: (0,-1), (1,0), (0,0), (1,0), (0,1)
E2: (-1,0), (0,-1), (2,0), (0,1), (-1,0)
```

The same map holds for both curves. The quotient involutions iota1 (z->-z) and iota2 (z->-z, y->-y) satisfy iota1·iota2=hyperelliptic=-1 on H1, so iota1+iota2=0. Since phi_k^*phi_k*=1+iota_k, this gives the polarization identity

```text
(phi1* x . phi1* y)_E1 + (phi2* x . phi2* y)_E2 = 2 (x . y)_C .
```

On all 25 pairs of chain cycles it holds exactly when the orientation signs (sigma, eps1, eps2) all agree. Each of the six mixed sign choices fails, by 2 or 4. Together with Riemann positivity, this pins every orientation, and the elliptic moduli tau_E1=W2/W1 and tau_E2=w2/w1 independently have positive imaginary part.

## Omega from the two elliptic blocks

Rebuilding A and B from the elliptic lattices and the integer map alone reproduces Omega; the four ball differences all contain zero, with radii at most 4.3 times 10^-58. Symbolically, the integer map forces

```text
Omega = [[-2/tau1, -1/tau1], [-1/tau1, (tau2 - 1/tau1)/2]],   tau_k = tau_{E_k},
```

so Omega11-2 Omega12 is certified to contain zero. This explicitly exhibits the (2,2) splitting in a period matrix, through a certified homology map rather than a differential projector. As an independent check of the E1 block, its segment periods equal pi/AGM(sqrt(u3-u1), sqrt(u3-u2)) and pi/AGM(sqrt(u3-u1), sqrt(u2-u1)) to the full ball precision.

## Controlled approach to a node, and monodromy composition

In the family Q=(u-1)(u-4)(u-4-delta), the branch points 2 and sqrt(4+delta) collide, as do their mirrors. Both gamma_5 and gamma_1 vanish, and gamma_4 crosses gamma_5 once. As delta circles zero, sqrt(4+delta) makes one full turn around 2. That is the square of a half-twist, and by the Birman–Hilden correspondence each half-twist lifts to the Dehn twist T_v. The local monodromy is therefore T_v^2, so

```text
P(gamma_4) = -(P(gamma_5)/(pi i)) log delta + holomorphic.
```

The certified data confirm this:

- For dz/y the regularized value is -1.51633924..., -1.51757107..., -1.51769447..., -1.51770681..., -1.51770805..., -1.51770817..., -1.51770818... at delta=10^-2 down to 10^-8. Each step shrinks by a factor of ten, as O(delta) requires.
- The coefficient P(gamma_5)/(2 pi i), which a single twist would give, drifts linearly in log delta instead.
- Fitting the logarithm with a free coefficient gives -2.0000002 times P(gamma_5)/(2 pi i) at delta=10^-8.
- For z dz/y the regularized value converges to -2.2350389... in the same way.

The two vanishing cycles compose as follows. b1=gamma_2+gamma_4 meets gamma_1 and gamma_5 with intersection numbers -1 and +1, so T_{gamma_1}^2 T_{gamma_5}^2 sends b1 to b1-2 gamma_1+2 gamma_5. The mirror vanishing periods are certified equal for dz/y and opposite for z dz/y. Consequently the dz/y period of b1 has no logarithm (its ball contains zero for every delta), while the z dz/y period of b1 regularizes to -4.4700779..., twice the single-node value.

## Scope

The packet is certified for two explicit curves with real branch points; the integer cycle map and the polarization identity are verified on them, not proved for every split curve. The node statement compares certified values at finitely many delta. It establishes the T_v^2 coefficient numerically to six digits and the O(delta) remainder empirically; it does not certify the whole asymptotic expansion. Complex branch-point configurations, automatic cycle marking for general sextics and the positive-resonance Legendre case remain open. The certified layer requires python-flint (Arb), installed with `pip install python-flint`; its tests skip without it.

```sh

```

The receipt is receipts/curve_structure/genus2_certified_periods.json. Four tests cover:

- the Riemann relations;
- the cycle map and the failure of a wrong-sign polarization;
- Omega rebuilt from the quotients;
- the AGM and contour checks, with an excluded-root guard;
- the node monodromy, including the failure of the single-twist coefficient.
