# P15 — Separate lattice information from chamber information

**Dependencies:** P08 and P14. **Objective:** identify sufficient preservation conditions for the marked series and diagnose why energy preservation alone can fail.

**Inspect first:** `docs/chapters/02_graphs_lattices.md`, `11_archival_bridges.md`, `13_research_program.md` §13.4; `src/singularity/graph.py`, `heights.py`; `docs/references.bib`. Established cut-lattice theory is background, not an originality claim.

**Work:** define the integral height quotient modulo constants and its energy lattice for connected graphs. Define the minimum-zero representative's volume and span as functions on that quotient. State a sufficient theorem: a lattice bijection preserving energy and both marking functions induces equality of marked populations, with coefficient finiteness justified. Investigate which concrete lattice maps preserve these functions; distinguish a linear lattice isometry from the nonlinear minimum-zero representative operation.

**Mathematical acceptance:** exhibit a nontrivial worked map or a certified failure of a proposed weaker condition. A vertex permutation is a useful baseline but cannot be the only evidence for a claim of new structure. If energy is preserved while volume changes, exhibit an explicit vector and compute both normalized marks. State separately what is known for the graphic matroid.

**Verification:** add proposed `tests/test_lattice_marks.py` for chosen integer maps, checking inverse identities and quadratic forms exactly; enumerate bounded vectors as diagnostics, not proof of universal preservation. Link any separating graphs to certificates.

**Done:** precise theorem, proved hypotheses and an instructive worked instance or counterexample. **Typical failure:** inferring height-volume preservation from equality of abstract theta series or from a cut-lattice isometry alone.
