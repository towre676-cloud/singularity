# P20 — Build the disjoint order-chamber formula

**Dependencies:** P02 and P06. **Objective:** enumerate the same profiles by a third, order-geometric representation with no boundary overcount.

**Inspect first:** `docs/chapters/13_research_program.md` §13.6; `src/singularity/heights.py`, `graph.py`; P02's independent chain enumerator. Proposed `src/singularity/chambers.py` and `tests/test_chambers.py` are new modules.

**Work:** sort vertices first by height and then ascending label. For a permutation \(\pi\), define gaps \(d_0=a_{\pi_1}\), \(d_j=a_{\pi_{j+1}}-a_{\pi_j}\). Require \(d_0\ge0\), \(d_j\ge0\), and \(d_j>0\) whenever \(\pi_j>\pi_{j+1}\). Prove this convention gives disjoint chambers covering every nonnegative tuple exactly once. Derive volume \(nd_0+\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}(n-j)d_j\) and energy by summing squared gap sums across graph edges.

**Mathematical acceptance:** implement the chamber histogram independently of composing heights; setting \(d_0=0\) gives normalized profiles, and span is \(\sum_{j\ge1}d_j\). Derive the quadratic cross coefficients explicitly and connect them to nested boundaries. State the factorial permutation cost honestly.

**Verification:** compare full local and marked histograms with compositions and chains on small graphs; emphasize all-zero profiles, repeated heights, label permutations and a profile with several tied blocks. Independently reconstruct each tuple's unique chamber.

**Done:** bijection proof, exact enumerator and complete three-way checks. **Typical failure:** summing closed weak-order cones, which counts tied profiles repeatedly, or dropping descent strictness during symmetry reduction.
