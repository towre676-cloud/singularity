Existing signatures already distinguish detected differences from agreement through truncation. Extend that discipline to graph populations and parameter strata.

**Work:** add proposed `src/singularity/visibility.py` and `tests/test_visibility.py`. Define equivalence through volume \(R\) by equality of every coefficient polynomial at degrees zero through \(R\). Define separate marked and specialized variants. Produce deterministic class identifiers, graph memberships, first-splitting volumes and exact discrepancy witnesses. Incorporate P24's algebraic exceptional parameters with an explicit coefficient domain in each record.

**Mathematical acceptance:** prove the partitions refine monotonically as volume increases, that a reported first difference has all lower coefficients equal, and that specialization can merge symbolic classes. `None` from the shared finite range is unresolved beyond that range, never infinite equality. State behavior when input truncation lengths differ and preserve signs of pairwise differences.

**Verification:** recover the spectral depth two, binary-pair depth three and cube–Möbius-ladder depth four examples. Test refinement on the complete declared census, duplicate graphs, relabelings and artificial exceptional parameters. Release every split witness and bound.
