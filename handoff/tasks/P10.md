# P10 — Prove safe volume truncation

**Dependencies:** P09. **Objective:** justify every intermediate pruning operation and connect bounded heights to unrestricted coefficients.

**Inspect first:** `src/singularity/transfer.py`: `_convolve`, `_validate_bounds`; `tests/test_transfer.py`: `test_volume_pruning_matches_unpruned_population`, `test_low_coefficients_equal_unbounded_composition_engine`; `scripts/benchmark_transfer.py`, `results/transfer_benchmark.json`. Existing pruning and benchmarks are evidence to audit, not a substitute for the theorem.

**Work:** prove that deleting monomials of volume greater than \(R\) after each sum or product preserves all requested coefficients because every subsequent contribution has nonnegative volume. Formalize the equivalence with computing in the quotient by \(x^{R+1}\). Prove that every nonnegative tuple of volume at most \(R\) has each height at most \(R\), hence `height=R` suffices for unrestricted coefficients through \(R\). State behavior when the default bounded-box volume cap is smaller.

**Mathematical acceptance:** all messages retain the same complete coefficient range and joint energy information; the argument must cover disconnected component convolution and minimum-zero subtraction. A time comparison with a different output population does not establish acceleration.

**Verification:** rerun the existing complete pruned/unpruned tests, then add a deliberately sparse high-energy example and a disconnected forest. For larger paths, check volume marginals independently using coefficients of \((1+x+\cdots+x^H)^n\); record dimensions, support sizes and environment.

**Done:** theorem, implementation correspondence and reproducible matched-output benchmarks. **Typical failure:** applying the argument to a representation with negative volume exponents or reporting the full height cube as the retained truncated population.
