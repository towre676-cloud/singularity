# P16 — Test Whitney switches systematically

**Dependencies:** P15. **Objective:** determine exactly how the height marks behave under a documented matroid-preserving operation.

**Inspect first:** P15's preservation theorem; `docs/chapters/13_research_program.md` §13.4; `src/singularity/graph.py`, `heights.py`, `certificates.py`; `scripts/census.py`. There is no switch-family generator yet. Proposed `scripts/whitney_switches.py` and `tests/test_whitney_switches.py` are new work.

**Work:** define two edge-disjoint two-terminal fragments and both identifications of their terminals. Specify hypotheses guaranteeing the resulting graphs are simple, connected and related by the intended Whitney switch. Retain fragment labels, maps to final labels and a structural witness. Independently check cycle-matroid preservation, for example by a proved cycle correspondence or complete circuit comparison on small instances. Do not assume any arbitrary edge rewiring is a Whitney switch.

**Mathematical acceptance:** either produce a certified marked-series separation explaining which volume/span information changes, or formulate a narrowly scoped transformation conjecture supported by explicit bounded comparisons. For possible equality, seek a uniform height bijection; finite agreement remains unresolved at its tested bound.

**Verification:** deduplicate isomorphic inputs without discarding transformation witnesses; compare exact local and marked coefficients with two enumerators where feasible. Release every tested pair, bounds, first discrepancies and unresolved cases. Confirm claimed nonisomorphism independently.

**Done:** reproducible family, checked matroid hypotheses and a structural result or precise conjecture. **Typical failure:** reporting no observed difference as universal matroid invariance, or allowing merged parallel edges to change the simple-graph energy without recording it.
