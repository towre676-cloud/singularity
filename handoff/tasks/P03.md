# P03 — Publish portable coefficient certificates

**Dependencies:** P01–P02. **Objective:** stabilize an auditable interchange contract and independently replay every mathematical sector.

**Inspect first:** `src/singularity/certificates.py`, `polynomials.py`; `tests/test_analytics.py` class `CertificateTests`; `results/certificates/`; `docs/chapters/12_computational_methodology.md`. The authoritative implemented format is **`singularity.certificate/1`**. The roadmap's proposed `relational-height/1` label is stale: document the discrepancy and reconcile the roadmap through a reviewed compatibility note, not a breaking silent rename.

**Work:** add proposed `docs/specifications/CERTIFICATE-v1.md` covering graph canonicalization, digest bytes, inclusive bound, local/normalized/marked counts, cumulative profile count, Euler exponent triples and optional comparison factors. Separate integer count pairs from rational polynomial triples. Extend independent replay to use P02's chain data as an additional check without making either enumerator depend on the other. Specify unknown-field and resource-budget behavior.

**Mathematical acceptance:** replay recomputes counts and verifies normalization, threshold energy and declared factorization; a digest or success flag is never proof. Reject malformed or negative exponents, duplicate terms, invalid graph edges, zero rational denominators and booleans impersonating integers.

**Verification:** retain `test_tamper_each_mathematical_sector`, `test_source_checks_and_budget`, and `test_boolean_count_cannot_impersonate_integer_one`; add malformed-record fixtures and a separately invoked replay command. Rebuild and replay every released certificate.

**Done:** documented compatible schema, deterministic fixtures, explicit trust boundary and mutation-test evidence. **Typical failure:** accepting JSON equality where `true == 1`, or changing released certificate semantics to match a proposed name.
