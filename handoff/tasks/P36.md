# P36 — Choose the second paper from actual results

**Dependencies:** P19, P22, P31–P32 and P35. **Objective:** choose one coherent contribution after the exploratory paths produce evidence.

**Inspect first:** the completed result records from Gauss sums, reciprocity, structural collisions and family bounds; `docs/chapters/13_research_program.md`, `14_literature_and_claims.md`; P35's claim register and reproducibility package. The six research paths are options for selection, not six obligatory sections of one paper.

**Work:** prepare a decision table listing each candidate's central theorem, novelty relative to verified primary literature, proof completeness, algorithmic usefulness, decisive negative results and remaining dependencies. Choose one center: for example a proved separator method with meaningful complexity bounds, a marked-lattice obstruction, an exact arithmetic classification, a genuine weighted reciprocity result or a uniform visibility family theorem. Create proposed `docs/papers/second_paper.md` only after this choice, with a title and abstract matching actual results.

**Mathematical acceptance:** provide a complete central proof and all finite artifacts needed to reproduce its examples. If no path has a coherent theorem or decisive negative classification, record that outcome and keep the paper unclaimed. Update the open-problem ledger with the strongest precise statements still unresolved and the evidence motivating them.

**Verification:** have an independent agent review assumptions and literature positioning; reproduce decisive computations from a clean checkout and inspect the rendered manuscript. Keep original regression pairs and normalizations stable.

**Done:** one focused paper package or a documented evidence-based decision to defer it, with an updated research agenda. **Typical failure:** combining unfinished paths to inflate length, or treating a useful enumerator alone as a broad structural breakthrough.
