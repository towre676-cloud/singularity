# P06 — Derive universal low-volume formulas

**Dependencies:** P04–P05. **Objective:** turn isolated calculations into formulas valid for every simple graph.

**Inspect first:** `docs/chapters/04_observation_hierarchy.md`, `09_exact_examples.md`, `10_inverse_problems.md`; `src/singularity/graph.py`, `heights.py`, `invariants.py`; `tests/test_core.py`. Some low-volume formulas are already stated. Verify their full hypotheses and extend their presentation rather than inventing incompatible conventions.

**Work:** create a proposition table for volumes zero through three. Partition profiles by integer partitions of the volume: `1`, `2`, `1+1`, `3`, `2+1`, `1+1+1`. Define degree multiset counts, ordered adjacent degree-pair counts and induced triple statistics independently of the target coefficients. For `2e_i+e_j`, derive \(4d_i+d_j-4A_{ij}\); for a binary triple, derive \(d_i+d_j+d_k-2e(G[\{i,j,k\}])\). Specify ordered versus unordered sums and empty-index conventions.

**Mathematical acceptance:** every profile appears once in a disjoint classification; resulting expressions equal the complete coefficient polynomials. Prove any regular/triangle-free simplification with its exact order assumptions. Distinguish local coefficients from minimum-zero coefficients when volume reaches the vertex count.

**Verification:** add proposed `tests/test_low_volume.py`, comparing formulas to compositions and P02 over all simple graphs through four vertices and selected larger regular graphs. Include \(n=1,2,3\), where translation subtraction affects low degrees.

**Done:** universal formulas, independently defined statistics, edge-case proofs and complete finite comparisons. **Typical failure:** defining a statistic as “the unexplained coefficient,” or using unordered pair counts in an ordered `2+1` sum.
