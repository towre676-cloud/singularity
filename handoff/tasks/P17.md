# P17 — Add exact volume congruence filters

**Dependencies:** P03. **Objective:** isolate volume residue classes by a mathematically exact character filter.

**Inspect first:** `src/singularity/conductor.py`: `CyclotomicRing`; `src/singularity/heights.py`, `polynomials.py`; `docs/chapters/13_research_program.md` §13.5; `tests/test_conductor_weighted.py`. The current cyclotomic machinery specializes energy, not volume. Reuse its exact ring arithmetic without conflating the two operations.

**Work:** add proposed `src/singularity/congruence.py` and `tests/test_congruence.py`. For modulus \(m\ge1\), residue \(a\), and a finite series, implement direct degree filtering and a genuinely independent character average. Prove \(m^{-1}\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\omega^{-aj}L(\omega^j x,q)=\sum_{v\equiv a\pmod m}[x^v]L\,x^v\). Support exact polynomial coefficients in \(q\), clearly defining how rational division in the average is represented.

**Mathematical acceptance:** cancellation in the cyclotomic ring yields the same integer coefficient series as modular assignment filtering. Specify residue normalization, modulus one and empty retained classes. The operation cannot recover span or binary profiles once those marks have been summed away.

**Verification:** compare direct coefficient filtering, character averaging and direct tuple enumeration on complete small graph populations. Include nonprimitive powers \(\omega^j\), multiple moduli and truncations below the modulus. Malformed or zero modulus must fail explicitly.

**Done:** theorem, exact APIs, independent three-way checks and semantic documentation. **Typical failure:** using floating-point roots of unity, confusing an energy conductor with a volume filter, or introducing fractional output counts after an incomplete character sum.
