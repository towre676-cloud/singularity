# P04 — Give the spectral pair a short coefficient proof

**Dependencies:** P03. **Objective:** make the first seven-vertex separation understandable and independently derivable without trusting a table.

**Inspect first:** `src/singularity/examples.py` (`spectral_g`, `spectral_h`), `invariants.py`, `heights.py`; `docs/chapters/04_observation_hierarchy.md`, `09_exact_examples.md`; `tests/test_core.py`: `DiscoveryTests.test_spectral_pair`. Preserve the released vertex labels and edge lists.

**Work:** audit the existing proof and add a compact derivation with a table of profile types through volume two. Volume zero is the zero profile; volume one consists of unit vectors; volume two consists of `2e_i` and `e_i+e_j`, with the latter split by adjacency. Derive their energies as \(4d_i\) and \(d_i+d_j-2A_{ij}\). List the degree-pair adjacency multiplicities that differ between the graphs. Compute the exact integer Laplacian characteristic polynomial independently of a floating-point eigensolver.

**Mathematical acceptance:** prove equality below volume two and the signed discrepancy \(2q^2(1-q)^3(1+q)\), with a declared graph order. Match its expanded coefficients and the value \(3/32\) at \(q=1/2\). Explain that degree assortativity already detects this example.

**Verification:** compare the hand classification with both P02 enumeration and compositions; replay the portable pair certificate. Retain the characteristic-polynomial regression assertion.

**Done:** a short readable proof, exact auxiliary tables and certificate are cross-linked in the manuscript. **Typical failure:** implying a new higher-order phenomenon when the difference is already explained by adjacent degree statistics, or reversing the pair without reversing the sign.
