# P27 — Produce controlled real singularity experiments

**Dependencies:** P26. **Objective:** illustrate the proved real asymptotic with honest numerical enclosures and a reproducible error budget.

**Inspect first:** `src/singularity/arithmetic.py`, `euler.py`; `results/exact_examples.json`; `docs/chapters/07_visibility_singularities.md`; P26's hypothesis records. There is no certified prime-product experiment driver yet. Proposed `scripts/visibility_experiment.py` and `tests/test_visibility_experiment.py` are new work.

**Work:** evaluate the normalized product \(K_P(s,q)\) for the released pairs at exact rational \(q\), including \(q=1/2\). Use a complete prime list through declared cutoff \(P\). Bound local-series truncation, arithmetic rounding and the omitted prime tail separately. Implement outward interval arithmetic or use a pinned validated library supported by official documentation; ordinary high precision alone is not an enclosure. Record precision, cutoff, coefficient depth, radius and every constant.

**Mathematical acceptance:** provide an interval containing \(K(s,q)\) near \(s=1/r\), positive where claimed, and show the normalized ratio approaches the independently proved exponent. If the conservative tail is too wide for a useful conclusion, report that limitation and improve the bound rather than hiding it.

**Verification:** compare multiple cutoffs and precision settings with overlapping enclosures. Check a trivial identical-graph ratio, reversed-pair reciprocals and the two exact released discrepancy exponents.

**Done:** data, driver, proof-linked error intervals and one carefully captioned plot or table. **Typical failure:** identifying the exponent by fitted slopes, reporting digits unsupported by the error budget, or ignoring local height-series truncation error.
