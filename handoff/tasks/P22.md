# P22 — Establish one genuine reciprocity statement

**Dependencies:** P21. **Objective:** prove one narrowly stated reciprocity identity, or decisively refute a proposed weighted extension.

**Inspect first:** P21's poset specification and `orders.py`; `docs/chapters/13_research_program.md` §13.6; the precise primary references in `docs/references.bib`. Proposed `docs/results/order_reciprocity.md` and `tests/test_reciprocity.py` should record the actual result, including negative outcomes.

**Work:** choose a fixed order polytope or a disjoint chamber family. State whether the theorem concerns unweighted counts, boundary-refined counts or a transformed weighted generating function. A safe baseline is the standard fixed-polytope relation between its Ehrhart polynomial evaluated at negative integers and counts in its relative interior; reproduce hypotheses and prove the chosen specialization. Translate strict/weak inequalities and endpoints back to height language explicitly.

**Mathematical acceptance:** either complete a genuinely new weighted identity with all boundary terms and parameter substitutions, or construct a smallest exact counterexample showing why the tempting extension fails. A standard baseline theorem alone should be labeled established background, not new project output. Negative height-bound evaluation requires a proved algebraic object, not an executable enumeration at a negative bound.

**Verification:** compare complete small posets and bounded ranges independently, including singleton, chain and antichain cases. For a counterexample, release expanded exact polynomials demonstrating the failed equality; numerical disagreement alone is insufficient.

**Done:** full proof or certified counterexample, precise scope and updated open questions. **Typical failure:** writing a formal replacement \(H\mapsto-H\) in a weighted sum without defining its extension, or suppressing boundary contributions.
