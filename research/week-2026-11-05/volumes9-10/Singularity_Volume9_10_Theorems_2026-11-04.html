Why a rank calculation becomes a theorem about actual graphs

The realization proof extends the earlier reservoir construction to every cutoff at most eleven. Admissible seed cores satisfy 3|U|−2|E(U)|≥λ for every nonempty vertex subset. Their ordinary-count matrix is unitriangular, hence has an integral inverse. An integer core vector can therefore be isolated by a signed seed combination.

Attach missing seed half-edges to separate sites in a large 3-edge-connected reservoir assembled from the explicit girth-twelve cubic graph. Distinct sites are at distance at least eleven. A new leafless core through ten vertices cannot cross the reservoir: it would need either a twelve-cycle or a tree connecting two distant sites. Core counts are preserved, including bridge-containing patterns. Singleton and edge fillers equalize the two graph orders without scaling the core vector.

For unrestricted graphs, separate high-girth cap completions plus the core-free order identity 510−382−126=2 equalize every even discrepancy. This strengthens the realization statement to unit scale there as well. Each class has exact isolated target-measurement witnesses. Rational kernels for fewer measurements clear to integer core vectors and hence to actual compatible graph pairs.

The witness recipes can specify very large graphs. Their exact seed arithmetic and structural construction are certified; those large graphs were not allocated or exhaustively height-enumerated. Their orders are construction upper bounds, not minimum witness sizes.
