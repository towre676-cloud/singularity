What can we learn with fewer measurements?

Two physical datasets, three frozen evaluation phases, complete subset searches, and independent replay. No new hardware was required.

Result: A six-sensor hydraulic candidate gives a useful constrained signal, but the stronger full-sensor classifier remains best. The minimum two-sensor hydraulic panel and the reduced audio panels fail their declared transfer margins. Exact selection ties optimally pruned greedy. These studies support further measurement-design research without establishing a general superiority claim.

The data are physical; the experiment is offline

The complete UCI hydraulic corpus contains 2,205 measured 60-second load cycles from an experimentally varied hydraulic rig. Fourteen physical sensor channels are eligible. Four component conditions vary simultaneously. Three computed virtual channels and the stability flag are excluded from diagnostic inputs. The source is Helwig, Pignanelli and Schütze, DOI10.24432/C5CW21, released under CC BY 4.0. The official 76,601,704-byte archive is SHA-256 pinned.

The MAFAULDA source provides synchronized tachometer, microphone and six accelerometer-axis measurements on a physical machinery-fault trainer. The bounded study acquires 42 complete five-second recordings, totaling 279,004,318 compressed signal bytes, covering normal operation, horizontal misalignment at 1.5 mm and imbalance at 6 g. The tachometer is excluded from diagnosis. This selected subset is not the full 1,951-record benchmark. The official UFRJ source and exact source-member identities accompany the features. Raw recordings are reacquired rather than redistributed.

A split that tests unfamiliar configurations

Every hydraulic cycle sharing the same four-component severity tuple stays in one partition. A deterministic hash order allocates 86 combinations to training, 28 to selection and 30 to final test: 1,575, 305 and 325 cycles respectively. Each component severity remains represented in all three partitions. This tests new combinations of familiar severities on the same rig; acquisition-session identities and independent machines are unavailable. No neighboring-window random split is used.

Nineteen fixed full-cycle descriptors per sensor feed training-only, class-balanced per-sensor ExtraTrees classifiers. Their clipped log probabilities are added across the selected sensors. Every one of 16,384 sensor masks is evaluated on selection data. The required balanced accuracy for each component is the full-panel selection score minus exactly 1/50. Costs prioritize sensor count, then logged scalar samples, then a deterministic mask tie break. Classification uses recorded floating-point predictions; feasibility and finite optimization use exact rational counts. Adding a sensor is not assumed to improve classification.

The minimum feasible selection panel is FS1+TS1: a flow sensor and a temperature sensor. Greedy and optimally pruned greedy select exactly the same pair. On test, its accumulator balanced accuracy falls to 59.02%, versus 65.97% for the full additive model. That 6.96-percentage-point loss exceeds the declared two-point preservation margin. The high cooler score and favorable average cannot replace this failed component-level requirement.

The separately declared erasure requirement retains the original selection threshold after deleting any one selected sensor. Its minimum panel is PS3, EPS1, FS1, FS2, TS2 and VS1: pressure, motor power, two flow channels, temperature and vibration. It uses six of fourteen sensors and 13,320 rather than 43,500 logged scalar samples per cycle, reductions of 57.14% and 69.38% under this accounting. Held-out mean component balanced accuracy is 86.33%, versus 81.03% for the same additive model with all sensors. Joint classification rises from 25.23% to 44.62%. A stronger all-feature ExtraTrees reference achieves 89.50% and 53.54%, so the six-sensor result does not establish global superiority.

The erasure guarantee is exact on the frozen selection table, not every future record. On held-out data, deleting FS1 reduces pump balanced accuracy to 85.17%, below the full additive model's 89.80% minus two points. Every measured erasure case and its confusion matrices is retained. The full-cycle recording duration remains 60 seconds; scalar-count savings are not measured latency, energy or hardware-price savings.

Audio transfer exposed a fragile optimum

The original audio study holds whole nominal-speed groups apart, with 12 training, six selection and six test recordings. Exact enumeration of 127 nonempty masks selects one underhang axial channel. Greedy and optimally pruned greedy tie it. The channel passes all six selection records and only two of six test records. Seven channels get six of six; microphone-only gets four of six. A second one-channel development optimum exists, making the deterministic tie choice a source of fragility. Selecting that alternative after seeing test scores would not be valid confirmation.

A new protocol therefore freezes the union of all optimal development singleton panels and fixed comparators, then acquires eighteen entirely new records at intervening speed groups. The original training and nearest-neighbor model remain unchanged. The original singleton gets 12/18, the two-channel union 15/18, the full seven channels 16/18, and microphone-only 16/18. The union improves the brittle singleton but misses the preservation margin and does not beat the simple microphone comparator. No exact-selection advantage is established. The original frozen evidence remains intact, including a metadata erratum clarifying that integrity decompression preceded its freeze while numeric parsing and modelling followed it.

What the mathematics actually certifies

After predictions are fixed, balanced accuracy is a finite rational sum of classwise correct counts. Exhausting every mask therefore establishes the minimum declared cost satisfying the selection inequalities. There are 3,311 ordinary-feasible and 69 erasure-feasible hydraulic masks. An independent checker rebuilds every mask's predictions by direct sensor-score sums, checks 65,536 rational task entries, and recomputes the optimum, restricted greedy pruning, erasure constraints and test metrics without importing the study selector. A deterministic refit reproduces all 56 training models' selection and test score tables bit for bit in the recorded environment. Separate audio implementations independently replay distances, subset optima, split identities and predictions; raw-to-feature replay also passed.

A supplementary, explicitly post-hoc diagnostic feeds empirical per-class feature envelopes into Singularity's existing robust-coverage engine. Twelve of eighteen severity-pair distinctions have overlapping development min-max boxes across every individual feature coordinate. Branch-and-bound and the independent exhaustive oracle agree that this conservative envelope certificate is infeasible. Product boxes discard correlations; their overlap is not a proof that the physical systems are indistinguishable. It explains why a universal noise-bound interpretation would exceed the available evidence.

Uncertainty intervals resample entire hydraulic fault combinations. Accumulator pressure 90 has only two test combinations, and some bootstrap samples omit classes as declared. The intervals are exploratory rather than noninferiority certificates. Both datasets come from single rigs, and the audio pilot is small. These measurements support fixed-channel replay, not unrecorded camera angles, microphone locations, active excitations or new-machine guarantees.

Original MIMII is the strongest next microphone-array candidate because its author release retains eight recording channels and multiple machine identities; corresponding noise mixtures must stay grouped. MIMII DUE offers explicit operating/environmental domain shifts. Real-IAD supplies a camera-view-selection route with five views per physical sample, provided all views stay grouped and original access requirements are satisfied. These sources were researched, not evaluated here. No images or video were acquired in this study.

The justified next step is a larger domain-held-out experiment that selects against nuisance variation and retains a protected final confirmation set. The present work makes that test executable and independently reviewable. Its useful constrained hydraulic candidate, failed transfer cases and strong baselines remain together in the evidence package.
