MAFAULDA was selected for the immediate audio/vibration pilot because the official UFRJ dataset retains a tachometer, six accelerometer axes and a microphone synchronously for each complete five-second physical recording. Its 1,951 recordings cover normal, imbalance, horizontal and vertical misalignment and several bearing-fault configurations, with rotation-speed and severity metadata. The normal archive is 325,307,137 bytes and the full official download is approximately 13 GB. HTTP Range access made the frozen 42-recording study feasible with 279,004,318 compressed signal bytes. The selected subset covers three states and one severity per faulty state only. The official landing page does not specify an explicit dataset license, so raw members are not redistributed. Official description and download links: https://www02.smt.ufrj.br/~offshore/mfs/page_01.html .

The original MIMII release is the strongest next microphone-array candidate. Its primary record describes industrial valve, pump, fan and slide-rail sound collected with an eight-channel microphone array at 16 kHz and 16 bits, factory background-noise mixtures, and normal/anomalous states. Public version 1.0 contains four machine IDs (00, 02, 04 and 06) per type, despite the general description referring to seven product models. The stated license is CC BY-SA 4.0. Its 12 archives total about 100 GB and individual archives are roughly 6.9–10.9 GB; member-level ZIP retrieval could support bounded studies without downloading all archives. A valid experiment should keep corresponding source recordings and their noise mixtures together across splits. This dataset was researched but not executed in this task. Primary author release: https://zenodo.org/records/3384388 .

MIMII DUE is useful for testing transfer across operating and environmental conditions. The primary author record describes five machine types, six sections per type, and source/target domains with changes such as speed and noise. Its development archives are about 1.1–1.2 GB each and its license is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. It supports a stronger domain-shift evaluation, but the published anomaly task should not be silently recast as fully supervised sensor-placement evidence. This dataset was researched but not executed here. Primary source: https://zenodo.org/records/4740355 .

The DCASE 2020 Task 2 development derivative combines ToyADMOS and MIMII, but explicitly retains only the first channel of the original multichannel recordings. Its 10-second recordings are single-channel at 16 kHz, with official normal-only training and normal/anomalous test sets. It is suitable for anomaly-detection and recording-duration studies, while it cannot test microphone-array placement or channel selection. The stated derivative license is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. It was not executed. Primary source: https://zenodo.org/records/3678171 .

IDMT-ISA-ELECTRIC-ENGINE contains 2,378 mono sound files from three similar physical motors under good, broken and heavy-load operational states, with background-noise conditions. The author-hosted Zenodo metadata specify 44.1 kHz, 32-bit mono, a 1,527,246,272-byte archive and CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. These recordings are useful for acoustic feature and nuisance-condition studies, but mono data do not support microphone-array design and the motor/state relationship must be audited before treating motor identity as a holdout unit. HTTP Range support was confirmed for the official archive, but no signal members were acquired or evaluated. Primary release: https://zenodo.org/records/7551261 .

Original ToyADMOS offers four-microphone measurements and a very large normal/anomalous corpus, but the author release exceeds 440 GB and uses split 7z archives of roughly 8.5 GB per part. Its Zenodo license metadata indicate a separate noncommercial license; the included LICENSE.pdf is authoritative. It is a valuable long-term multichannel source but less convenient for a bounded immediate replay. It was not executed. Primary release: https://zenodo.org/records/3351307 and author repository https://github.com/YumaKoizumi/ToyADMOS-dataset .
