# Source attribution and derived measurement evidence

The original recordings are **MIMII Dataset: Sound Dataset for Malfunctioning Industrial Machine Investigation and Inspection**, public version 1.0, by Harsh Purohit, Ryo Tanabe, Kenji Ichige, Takashi Endo, Yuki Nikaido, Kaori Suefusa and Yohei Kawaguchi, Hitachi, Ltd., 2019. The official record is <https://zenodo.org/records/3384388>, DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3384388>. The primary paper is <https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09347>. The source is made available under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International**, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>.

The retained feature table and measurement-derived plots and prediction evidence are adaptations of that source and are supplied under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes consist of deterministic subset selection; whole-recording descriptor extraction; supervised training on IDs 00 and 02; selection on ID 04; protected evaluation on ID 06; finite microphone-subset enumeration; uncertainty and erasure diagnostics; and presentation of the resulting analyses. No source author endorsement is asserted.

Original audio is not redistributed. Acquisition scripts retrieve exact source members from the official release to a local cache, verify identities and checksums, and preserve their names and provenance. Publisher whole-archive MD5 metadata are retained for reference; selective acquisition does not verify a whole archive's checksum. Member CRC32 and SHA-256 checks are stated at their actual scope.

This attribution and derived-data notice applies to the measurement evidence and its adaptations. It does not relicense the rest of Singularity's code or unrelated historical experiments, and it does not impose the MIMII license on those separate works.
